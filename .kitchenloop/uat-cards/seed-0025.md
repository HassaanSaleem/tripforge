# User Test Card: seed-0025

## Feature (one sentence)
A traveler opening a shared link to a BOOKED trip sees the client prices the booking
confirmed (never the agency's lower net rates), and prices on un-booked shared trips are
visibly marked as estimates.

## Prerequisites
- [ ] The live stack is running at http://localhost:3000 (the implementer booted it)
- [ ] A browser is available via the Playwright MCP tools

## Prerequisite Verification Commands
```bash
curl -sf http://localhost:3000/api/health   # should print a JSON body containing "ok"
```

## Steps

### Step 1: Create an agency account
**Browser action:** navigate to http://localhost:3000/sign-up, fill "Your name" with
"Uma UAT", fill "Email" with "uma-uat-seed0025@example.test", fill "Password" with
"uat-seed0025-pw", fill "Agency name" with "Umbrella UAT Travel", click
"Create account & agency"
**Expected visible result:** "Plan a trip" (the workspace planner) and "Umbrella UAT Travel"
in the header
**Expected NOT visible:** "Invalid" or any error banner
**Screenshot:** save as step-1.png in the evidence directory

### Step 2: Draft the deterministic Paris trip
**Browser action:** on http://localhost:3000/workspace, fill "Destination" with "Paris",
fill "Check-in" with "2026-09-01", fill "Check-out" with "2026-09-04", fill "Budget" with
"1000", fill "Currency" with "USD", click "Draft itinerary", wait for the drafted plan
**Expected visible result:** "Paris balanced trip" and "Day subtotal: 198.60 USD"
**Expected NOT visible:** "Draft failed"
**Screenshot:** save as step-2.png in the evidence directory

### Step 3: Quote at 22% markup
**Browser action:** in the "Book this trip at your markup" section, fill "Markup %" with
"22", click "Quote live prices", wait for the review table
**Expected visible result:** a "Client price" column containing "152.50 USD" and a
"Net rate" column containing "125.00 USD" on the "Paris Boutique" row, with "22%" markup
**Expected NOT visible:** "No items could be held"
**Screenshot:** save as step-3.png in the evidence directory

### Step 4: Book every held item
**Browser action:** click "Book selected items", wait for the confirmation
**Expected visible result:** "Booking confirmed", a "Booking reference:" line, and the
line containing "Paris Boutique" ends with "152.50 USD"
**Expected NOT visible:** "failed"
**Screenshot:** save as step-4.png in the evidence directory

### Step 5: Share the booked trip with the client
**Browser action:** click "Share with client" in the "Share with your client" section,
wait for the share URL to appear, and record the full http://localhost:3000/view/... URL
shown
**Expected visible result:** a URL matching http://localhost:3000/view/ followed by a
36-character token
**Expected NOT visible:** "Could not"
**Screenshot:** save as step-5.png in the evidence directory

### Step 6: Open the share link as the traveler — booked prices, not net rates
**Browser action:** navigate to the exact URL recorded in Step 5
**Expected visible result:** "Trip total" with "424.31 USD"; "152.50 USD" on the
"Paris Boutique" row; "Booked" badges on the items
**Expected NOT visible:** "347.80" (the agency's internal net-rate total); "125.00 USD"
(the agency's net rate for the stay); "estimate"; "TripForge"
**Screenshot:** save as step-6.png in the evidence directory

### Step 7: Draft and share a SECOND trip WITHOUT booking — estimates say so
**Browser action:** navigate to http://localhost:3000/workspace, fill "Destination" with
"Lisbon", fill "Check-in" with "2026-10-05", fill "Check-out" with "2026-10-08", fill
"Budget" with "1500", fill "Currency" with "USD", click "Draft itinerary", wait for the
drafted plan, then click "Share with client", record the new /view/... URL, and navigate
to it
**Expected visible result:** "Trip total" followed by "includes estimates", and the word
"estimate" beside the item prices
**Expected NOT visible:** "Booked"; "TripForge"
**Screenshot:** save as step-7.png in the evidence directory

### Step 8: Bad link is refused gracefully
**Browser action:** navigate to
http://localhost:3000/view/00000000-0000-4000-8000-000000000000
**Expected visible result:** "Your trip" heading and "isn't available"
**Expected NOT visible:** "424.31"; "TripForge"; any stack trace or "500"
**Screenshot:** save as step-8.png in the evidence directory

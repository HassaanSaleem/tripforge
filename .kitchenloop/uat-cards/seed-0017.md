**Stack**: http://localhost:3000 (built compose image, already booted for you;
keyless — the planner is deterministic). Drive a real browser; screenshot every
step. Do not modify any product file.

**Feature under test**: `/workspace` now has a "Your trips" panel listing the
agency's itineraries, with an open-in-place "Continue work" view.

## Steps

1. Open `http://localhost:3000/sign-up`. Fill: Your name `Uma Tester`, Email
   `uat-seed0017-a@example.test`, Password `uat-pass-seed0017`, Agency name
   `UAT Alpha Seed0017`. Click **Create account & agency**.
   **Expect**: you land on `/workspace`.

2. On `/workspace`, below the "Plan a trip" planner card, find the panel with
   eyebrow **Your trips** and heading **Trips in your workspace**.
   **Expect**: an empty state whose text begins `Nothing here yet.`, mentions
   drafting with the planner, and contains a link named `marketplace`. No trip
   rows. Screenshot.

3. In the planner form fill: Destination `Paris`, Check-in `2026-09-01`,
   Check-out `2026-09-04`, Adults `2`, Children `0`, Trip style `balanced`,
   Budget `1000`, Budget mode `hardCap`, Currency `USD`. Click **Draft
   itinerary** and wait (up to 30 s) for the drafted itinerary to render.
   **Expect**: a drafted itinerary titled `Paris balanced trip` with Total
   `1,000.00 USD`; AND — WITHOUT reloading the page — the "Trips in your
   workspace" panel now shows exactly ONE row: title `Paris balanced trip`, a
   `USD` badge, a human-readable date such as `11 Jul 2026` (never a raw
   ISO timestamp), and an **Open** button. The `Nothing here yet.` text is
   gone. Screenshot.

4. Reload the page (`/workspace`).
   **Expect**: the same single `Paris balanced trip` row is still listed.

5. Click **Open** on the row.
   **Expect**: the panel is replaced by a **Continue work** view: a **← Back
   to trips** button, heading `Paris balanced trip`, day sections `Day 1 —
   2026-09-01`, `Day 2 — 2026-09-02`, `Day 3 — 2026-09-03` each with priced
   items in USD, an "Inline booking" section with a **Quote live prices**
   button, and a **Share with client** button. Screenshot.

   **Expect**: the list view returns with the same single row.
